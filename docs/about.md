## Licensing

The compendium's own work (introductions, notes and new translations) is
released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You
may copy, adapt and republish it for any purpose, as long as you credit the
Manichaean Scripture Compendium.

Older translations are included only when they are in the public domain in
both the United States and the European Union, and each one is marked as such.
Modern copyrighted translations are consulted as references and are never
reproduced.

## Plan

1. Public-domain English translations: the Uyghur confession of the hearers
   (Le Coq, 1911), and Mani's words as quoted by al-Bīrūnī (Sachau, 1879) and
   Augustine (1887).
2. New translations from public-domain editions: the Chinese *Traité*, the
   Fihrist's account of Mani's doctrine, and early Turfan fragments.
3. New translations of the Coptic Psalm-Book, the Kephalaia and the Homilies,
   and of the Greek Cologne Mani Codex.
