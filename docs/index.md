# The scriptures of Mani, gathered and free

Manichaeism was founded by the prophet Mani in third-century Mesopotamia and
spread from the Roman West to China. Its scriptures were deliberately
translated into every language the church reached. Most are lost. What
survives does so in fragments: papyrus codices from Egypt, manuscripts from
the oasis of Turfan and the caves of Dunhuang, and quotations kept by the
religion's opponents.

This compendium gathers the surviving texts in English. It reproduces older
translations that are in the public domain, and makes new translations,
released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
where no free translation exists.
