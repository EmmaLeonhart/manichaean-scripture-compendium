## Introduction

This text is called the *Traité* after the title of its first edition,
Chavannes and Pelliot's "Un traité manichéen retrouvé en Chine" (1911), and
the *Sermon of the Light-Nous* after its subject. It survives in one Chinese
manuscript from Dunhuang, now in Beijing, which lacks its opening lines. The
Taishō canon prints it under the misleading title 波斯教殘經, "Fragmentary
Scripture of the Persian Religion". Parts of the same sermon survive in
Parthian and Sogdian, which shows that it was a Manichaean work translated
across the church's whole range.

In the dialogue, the Envoy of Light (明使) answers his disciple Adda (阿馱),
the apostle who took the faith to the Roman East. Adda asks how the light
bound in the body can be freed. The Envoy explains that the Demon of Greed
built the human body as a small copy of the universe, to be a prison for
the light, and that the Light-Nous (惠明, "Wise Light") comes into the
person to free it. The Light-Nous overthrows the "old man" and builds the
"new man", cutting down the five dead trees of the passions and planting
five living trees of virtue. The last part lists the twelve signs by which
a true member of the Elect can be recognised.

**This translation is a draft, made for this compendium from the Chinese.**
Square brackets [ ] mark gaps in the manuscript. Parentheses ( ) mark words
added for sense. Technical terms are given once with their Chinese.
Section headings are added. The translation is being published in parts.

## The translation

### 1. Adda's question

[…] if they do not meet with the right conditions, they have no means of
freeing themselves; seeking release […] the original nature of the body of
flesh: is it one, or is it two? All the holy ones who appear in the world
use skilful means and are able to save the light-nature, so that it leaves
all suffering behind and comes at last to peace and joy.

When he had asked this, he bowed low in reverence and stepped back to stand
to one side.

### 2. The building of the world as a prison for darkness

Then the Envoy of Light said to Adda: "Excellent, excellent! For the good of
countless beings you have been able to ask about so deep and secret a
meaning. You are now a great good friend to all the blind and lost beings of
the world. I will explain it to you part by part, so that the net of your
doubts is cut away for ever and nothing is left of it.

"Know this. Before this world was set up, the two Envoys of Light, the
Living Spirit and the Good Mother, went down into the dark pit, the realm of
ignorance. They drew up the valiant one, [the First Thought, with his]
armour of great wisdom, the fivefold body of light, and led him upward, and
brought him out of the five pits. But the five kinds of demons stuck to the
five bodies of light like flies stuck in honey, like birds caught with
lime, like fish that have swallowed the hook.

"For this reason the Living Spirit, Envoy of Light, took the two forces
mingled together, the five kinds of demons and the five bodies of light,
and made the world out of them: ten heavens and eight earths. So this world
is the medicine-hall where the body of light is healed, and also the prison
where the demons of darkness are held in chains.

"The Living Spirit and the Good Mother, with skilful means, set up the ten
heavens. Next they placed the wheel of deeds and the palaces of the sun and
moon, and below them the eight earths, the three garments, the three
wheels, and even the three calamities, the iron enclosure, the four walls,
Mount Sumeru (未勞俱孚山) and all the lesser mountains, the great sea and the
rivers. When they had made all this, they had built the world and shut up
the five kinds of demons. All of them are held prisoner by the thirteen
great powers of light.

"These thirteen great valiant powers are the five sons of light of the
First Thought and the five of the Living Spirit, together with Xrōštag and
Padvāxtag, and the Srōšahrāy. The five bodies of light are like the
prison, and the five kinds of demons like its prisoners. The five sons of
the Living Spirit are like the prison officers. The Call and the Answer (說聽,
喚應) are like the night watchmen calling the hours. The thirteenth, the
Srōšahrāy, is like the king who judges cases.

### 3. The Demon of Greed builds the human body as a copy of the world

"When the Demon of Greed saw this, she laid an evil plan once more in her
poisoned heart. She made Luxian (路㑥) and Yeluojue (業羅決) copy the Living
Spirit and the Good Mother, and by transformation among themselves they
built the human body. They imprisoned the light-nature in it and modelled
it on the great world. This poisonous, evil body of greed and lust, small
as it is, copies every part: heaven and earth, the world, the wheel of deeds
and the stars, the three calamities, the four walls, the great sea and the
rivers, the dry and the wet earths, plants and trees, birds and beasts,
mountains and streams, hills and mounds, spring, summer, autumn and winter,
years, months, hours and days, and even what is solid and what is not.
There is nothing in it that does not copy the world. It is like a goldsmith
who copies the shape of a white elephant and engraves it inside a ring: the
elephant's body is neither larger nor smaller (than the original). The human
world is the same.

"The Living Spirit took the five kinds of demons and imprisoned and bound
them in the thirteen pure bodies of light, and did not let them be free.
When the Demon saw this, she conceived a greedy and poisonous heart. She
imprisoned the five light-natures in the body of flesh as a small world,
and bound it fast with the thirteen dark powers of ignorance, and did not
let it be free.

### 4. The five prisons and the five dead trees

"The Demon of Greed imprisoned the pure Air in the city of bone, set dark
Sense there, and planted a dead tree. She imprisoned the wondrous Wind in
the city of sinew, set the dark Mind there, and planted a dead tree. She
imprisoned the Light in the city of the veins, set dark Thought there, and
planted a dead tree. She imprisoned the wondrous Water in the city of flesh,
set dark Reflection there, and planted a dead tree. She imprisoned the
wondrous Fire in the city of skin, set dark Intention there, and planted a
dead tree.

"The Demon of Greed planted these five poisonous dead trees in five kinds
of ruined ground. With them she always confuses the original nature of the
light, and draws out that stranger-nature (客性) and turns it into
poisoned fruit. The tree of dark Sense grows in the city of bone, and its
fruit is resentment. The tree of the dark Mind grows in the city of sinew,
and its fruit is anger. The tree of dark Thought grows in the city of the
veins, and its fruit is lust. The tree of dark Reflection grows in the city
of flesh, and its fruit is wrath. The tree of dark Intention grows in the
city of skin, and its fruit is folly.

"So the five, bone, sinew, veins, flesh and skin, are made into prisons to
hold the fivefold body, just as the five lights (of the cosmos) hold the
demons prisoner. And resentment, anger, lust, wrath and folly are made the
prison officers. In between she set the greed of the five valiant sons of
the Living Spirit, to copy the night watchmen, the Call and the Answer.
The fierce fire of gluttony she let run free, to copy the Srōšahrāy.

### 5. The cage of snakes

"When the five bodies of light have been held in such bitter bondage, they
forget their original mind, as though mad or drunk. It is as if someone
wove a cage out of poisonous snakes, all their heads turned inward,
spitting poison in every direction, and then took a man and hung him upside
down inside it. Pressed by the poison and hanging head down, his mind is
confused, and he has no leisure to think of his father and mother, his
family, or the joys he once had. The five light-natures in the body of
flesh, held prisoner by the Demon and suffering day and night, are just
the same.

### 6. The two ships and the Demon's counter-plot

"Then the Living Spirit made the two ships of light, to carry the good sons
across the sea of birth and death to their original realm, so that the
light-nature might come at last to peace and joy. When the Demon, the
enemy, the lord of greed, saw this, she became angry and jealous. She at
once made two forms, male and female, copying the two great ships of light,
the sun and moon, to lead the light-nature astray and make it board the
dark ships. These carry it into hell, to turn through the five paths of
rebirth, suffering every pain, hard to free at last.

### 7. The Light-Nous enters the old house

"But if an Envoy of Light appears in the world to teach beings and free
them from their sufferings, he first sends down the sound of the wondrous
Law through the gate of the ear. Then he enters the old house, holds the
great divine spell, binds the poisonous snakes and the evil beasts, and
does not let them be free. Then he brings the axe of wisdom, cuts down the
poisonous trees, digs out the stumps and the other weeds, and makes it all
clean. He adorns the palace, sets out the seat of the Law, and sits on it,
like a king who has defeated an enemy country and, inside it, adorns the
halls, sets up his jewelled throne, and judges all the people, good and
bad.

"The Light-Nous is the same. When he has entered the old city and broken
the enemy, he at once separates the two forces, light and darkness, so
that they are no longer mixed. First he subdues resentment and binds it in
the city of bone, so that the pure Air is freed from its bonds. Next he
subdues anger and binds it in the city of sinew, so that the wondrous Wind
is freed. Then he subdues lust and binds it in the city of the veins, so
that the Light is freed from its bonds. Then he subdues wrath and binds it
in the city of flesh, so that the wondrous Water is freed. Then he subdues
folly and binds it in the city of skin, so that the wondrous Fire is freed.
The two demons of greed and lust he binds in between, and the fierce fire
of hunger he lets run free.

"It is like a goldsmith refining gold: he must use fire, and without fire
the refining cannot be done. The Light-Nous is like the goldsmith, […] is
like the ore, and the Demon of Hunger is the fierce fire that refines the
fivefold body and makes it pure. In the bodies of the good, the great Envoy
Light-Nous uses the fire of hunger to great profit."

*(The translation continues in later updates: the three days and two
nights, the five battles of the old and new man, the Light-Nous in the five
cities, the dead and living trees, the king of virtues, the twelve signs of
the Elect, and the closing hymn of the teachers.)*

## Notes to this edition

- **The base text** is the Taishō edition (vol. 54, no. 2141B, pp. 1281a–1286a)
  as transcribed by CBETA, checked against the logic of the passage.
  Chavannes and Pelliot's French (1911, public domain) will be used to check
  the draft. The translation does not reproduce CBETA's notes or
  punctuation.
- **路㑥 and 業羅決** are the male and female demons who make Adam and Eve:
  Ašaqlūn and Nebrōēl in the Western sources. The Chinese renderings are
  transcriptions, given here in pinyin.
- **[…] in §7**: the characters 㘈𠺕而云䁥 are unclear. They seem to
  transcribe a foreign term for the mixed substance being refined. They are
  left untranslated until checked.
- **客性**, "stranger-nature": the light that is a guest in the body.
