## Introduction

These are small leaves and pieces from Müller's 1904 edition: two lists of
the twelve "glories" (aeons) of light and of darkness; lists of angels;
a number of the angels of the heavens; and hymns of praise to the Father of
Greatness, to Jesus at the new moon and the full moon, to Mani, and to the
god Naresaf (Narisah, the Third Envoy).

**This translation is a draft.** It is made from F. W. K. Müller's first
edition of 1904, the only public-domain edition, which he called "a first
attempt". Gaps are marked [ … ]; words Müller marked as guesses are marked
(?); words in parentheses are added for sense. Where Müller gave no
meaning for a word, it is given in italics.

## The translation

### M 14: the twelve glories of light

*Among other things, the names of the aeons.* The twelve Glories (the
aeons of light):

1. Lordship
2. Wisdom
3. Salvation
4. Contentment
5. Youth (?)
6. Righteousness
7. *hamdānēft*
8. Long-suffering (?)
9. *razwarēft*
10. Good action
11. *huabsagāft*
12. Light

### M 34: the twelve glories of darkness

*Among other things, the names of the aeons of darkness.* The twelve dark
Glories:

the first, evil knowledge; the second, greed; the third, *gahrādanēft*;
the fourth, confusion; the fifth, [ … ]; the sixth, fettering; the seventh,
*wigānišn*; the eighth, destruction (?); the ninth, death; the tenth, evil
[ … ]; the eleventh, oppression; and the twelfth, darkness.

### M 20: names of angels

*A fragment, the lower part of a small double leaf.*

[ … ] the demon. Jacob the angel; the lord Bar Simus; Qaftinus the mighty;
Raphael, Gabriel, Michael, Sarael; Narsus, Nastikus.

**Back.** [ … ] of the Father, forgiven, and [ … ]. *Invocation:* [ … ] our
voice [ … ]; for I am of the righteous, and not of the [ … ]

**Second leaf.** [ … ] may it be [ … ] see, and [ … ] make in this religion
[ … ] the holy one, the bride of the king; to receive the holy gift of the
Saviour (?) [ … ].

*(The back has only scattered words: "the Father", "and the way of the
religion", "the voice of our songs", "hear you", "guardian", "see the
power".)*

### M 33: the angels of the heavens

*A fragment, lower piece, front.*

[ … ] three hundred and eleven myriads and four hundred angels. Through
the course (?) of night and day, from each one [ … ] become [ … ] twenty
and four [ … ] the ten heavens become [ … ]

*Second column.* [ … ] the heavens [ … ] I lament (?) to your glory;
reverence to the lord Mani, by whom this mystery has been rightly taught
(?).

### M 309: the reward of the righteous

*A leaf fragment.*

[ … ] ruler [ … ] a new, shining throne from the Mother of the Living, a
new diadem, a golden crown, from [ … ] a new dwelling and [ … ] (the rest
is lost).

### M 8: a hymn with names of angels

*The upper half of a leaf. Red heading: "The angels".*

Blessing and praise to Narsus, Nastikus, and to Bar Zaqāh, to the blessed
angel (apostle?) [ … ] great weapon [ … ] (the second column and the back
are hard to read).

### M 40: a hymn to the Father

*The upper half of a small leaf. The front cannot be read. Back, red
heading: "The great ones".*

All-shining (?), first: praised and blessed are you, Father, living,
[ … ], who are the first and the highest; King of Light, [ … ] strongest
and mighty. You are the Father of all this work, of the "lords", the
"powers", the "lights" and the "hosts", as has been said: from you born,
from you sprung, from you come forth, from you revealed [ … ], through you
they become mighty [ … ] (the rest is lost).

### M 730: the twelve great ones

*The upper right corner of a larger leaf in two columns. Heading in
ornamental letters, incomplete.*

He holds twelve diadems of light. They stand before the twelve great ones,
the sons of insight (?), like the twelve shining faces of the Father of
light. Many *yazads*, *bags* and *radans* (princes?) of the Lord of
Paradise, blessed, called, answered, and [ … ] from them, from the twelve
great first-born, the kings and rulers [ … ]

**Back.** *(Badly preserved; the first three lines in red. Second
column:)* Mighty one of the gods, highest god [ … ], lord of the gods, most
divine! Praise to the god of the shining ones, the glory, the high rampart
of light, the blessed kingdom in which you dwell, pure and shining [ … ]
all full of joy, [ … ] trust, life [ … ] (end).

### M 176: hymns of the new moon and the full moon

*A leaf fragment, much worn and hard to read. Blue heading: "King …".*

[ … ] living [ … ] to us [ … ] new moon [ … ] Paradise and new joy came

Jesus, first of the gods! New moon, you are god, and you are [ … ], full

Jesus, O Lord, of growing fame! Full moon, O Jesus, Lord! [ … ] to all

Jesus, god (?), and Wahman! Splendour of god! Let us praise the god
Naresaf; let us bless Mār Mānī.

New full moon and spring, [ … ] Lord, let us bless [ … ] the angels, the
gods [ … ]

*(Another piece:)* [ … ] new sun [ … ] Mihr [ … ]

**Back.** *(Much worn, with a blue heading almost destroyed. A prayer.)*

May a new full moon shine on the religion! Welfare over you, O Lord! Come
with welfare, Lord, [ … ] peaceful (?) Mār Mānī, O god, save me! O god, save
me!

A new day has come, and new joy; a new day has come. Come, new [ … ] of the
living, full of joy. Holy Jesus, forgive my sins! God, Mār Mānī, save my
soul! Holy god!

O Light! Look on me! O Power! O Wisdom! O god, save me! [ … ] heard the
offering of this ruler and the voice of those who praise (?) [ … ] O
awakener (?), to you [ … ] chosen Son, chosen Son [ … ] (the rest is
destroyed).

### M 32: a hymn, and a hymn to the god Naresaf

*The upper half of a leaf, fairly well preserved. Heading: "Hymn".*

[ … ] who proclaim the Glory (the majesty). O crowned (?) Father, worthy
of the blessing of every mouth! O Son of the gods, who gives life to many!
O great Caller, who awakens (?) this soul of mine from slumber! O shining
lamp (?), who made my heart and eye bright! O perfect seal of my hand, my
mouth and my thought! O spear [ … ]; O good sword (?) [ … ] my wisdom and
sign [ … ] my eyes [ … ]

**Back.** *Heading: "The god Naresaf".*

Praise the great Light! Bring [ … ]; with one word let us give praise to
this Glory; let us become singers of praise, let us praise this Ruler, the
Father who does good, the guide (?) of souls, power and youth, so that you
may give to us. Let us give praise and make worship to his Greatness.
Blessed Father, beauty of light, in which there is no flaw [ … ] ruler of
all the kingdoms (aeons) [ … ] (end of the fragment).

## Notes to this edition

- **The twelve glories** (M 14, M 34) are the "twelve aeons" of the
  Father of Greatness and their dark counterparts. Several of the names
  are abstract nouns in *-ēft*, whose meaning Müller could not give.
- **The angels** (M 20, M 8, M 4): Müller notes that another passage, not
  yet found again in 1904, names Jacob, Seth, Bar Simus, Nukhael and
  Qaftinus.
- **"The seal of my hand, my mouth and my thought"** (M 32): the three
  "seals" of the Elect, which Augustine calls the *signacula oris et
  manuum et sinus*.
- **"O Light! O Power! O Wisdom!"** (M 176): Müller compares al-Nadīm's
  "God, his Light, his Power and his Wisdom", and the Greek abjuration
  formula that curses "the four-faced Father of Greatness".
- **Naresaf** (M 176, M 32): the god Narisah, the Third Envoy.
- **Wahman** (M 176): the Great Nous (the Light-Nous).
