# Scope of the compendium

The scope is defined by the English Wikipedia navbox
[Template:Manichaeism footer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Manichaeism_footer)
(user's instruction, 2026-10-20). Its "Scripture", "Extracanonical
literature" and "Visual art" groups are what the compendium covers. Anything
outside them is out of scope unless the user adds it.

Status key: **done** (on the site), **draft** (on the site, not yet checked),
**source** (base material downloaded), **—** (nothing yet).
