# Manichaean scripture compendium: research summary

## The question (assumption, written down at the start of work mode)

What survives of Manichaean scripture, in which languages and manuscripts?
For each text, is there a translation that can be reproduced freely, or does
it need a fresh CC BY 4.0 translation? And what is the order of work that gets
the most scripture online soonest? The user stated the goal ("a compendium of
Manichaean scripture. Released under Creative Commons. Translating again if
need be") but did not set a scope. This is my reading of it.

## Current picture (2026-10-20, before any sources were checked)

This starts from background knowledge. Every item below still needs checking
against sources in `research/sources.md`.

- **Mani's canon** had seven works (the Living Gospel, Treasure of Life,
  Pragmateia, Book of Mysteries, Book of Giants, Epistles, Psalms and
  Prayers), plus the Shabuhragan and the picture book (Ardahang). None
  survives whole. Fragments survive in quotations and in Turfan manuscripts.
- **The Coptic corpus** (Medinet Madi, found in 1929) includes the Psalm-Book,
  the Kephalaia of the Teacher, the Kephalaia of the Wisdom of my Lord Mani,
  the Homilies, and the Synaxeis, which is still largely unpublished.
- **The Greek Cologne Mani Codex** is a hagiography of Mani's early life that
  quotes his own writings.
- **Turfan and Dunhuang** texts are in Middle Persian, Parthian, Sogdian, Old
  Uyghur (including the Xwastwanift confession) and Chinese (the Hymnscroll,
  the Compendium of the Teachings, and the Traité/Pelliot fragment).
- **Kellis** (Egypt) has yielded Coptic and Greek letters and liturgical
  texts.
