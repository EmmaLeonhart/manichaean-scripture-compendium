# Sources

One entry per source: citation or link, date accessed, kind, what it
contributes, and how far to trust it.

- **NYPL, Catalog of Copyright Entries renewals** (transcription),
  github.com/NYPL/cce-renewals, files `data/1958-1.tsv` to `1961-1.tsv`.
  Accessed 2026-10-20. Primary record (a transcription of a government
  register). Contributes: Jackson, *Researches in Manichaeism* (1932) renewed
  R249768, 1960-01-04. Trust: high. A missing entry is weaker evidence than a
  found one.
- **Cambridge Core, JRAS 43.2 (April 1911)**, record for Le Coq's
  "Dr. Stein's Turkish Khuastuanift". Accessed 2026-10-20. Publisher
  catalogue. Contributes: the publication facts (pp. 277–314). Trust: high.
- **Radloff, review in *Mélanges asiatiques* 15 (1911–12)**,
  orientalstudies.ru PDF. Accessed 2026-10-20. Contemporary scholarly review.
  Contributes: confirms that Le Coq 1911 has an English translation and a
  supplement from Berlin fragments. Trust: high.
- **archive.org search**, accessed 2026-10-20. Scans found:
  `gtu_32400005908003_2` (Allberry 1938), `researchesinmani0000avwi` and
  `in.ernet.dli.2015.187154` (Jackson 1932), `maniseinelehreu00manegoog`
  (Flügel 1862). Trust: high for whether a scan exists, none for copyright.
- **Wikipedia, "Charles Allberry"**. Accessed 2026-10-20. Encyclopedia.
  Contributes: Allberry died in 1943. Trust: medium.
- **Library catalogues** (Columbia UP 1932 record for Jackson, with his dates
  1862–1937; Kohlhammer 1938 record for Allberry). Accessed 2026-10-20.
  Trust: high.

## Editions used for the translations of 2026-10-21

All accessed 2026-10-21 on archive.org (OCR text, `_djvu.txt`). Kind:
scholarly editions with translations. Public-domain basis: published before
1930 (US) and the editors died more than 70 years ago (EU), unless noted.
Trust: high for the texts as printed; the OCR is noisy, and every
translation made from it is a draft.

- **F. W. K. Müller, "Handschriften-Reste in Estrangelo-Schrift aus Turfan II"**,
  Abh. Preuss. Akad. 1904, in `abhandlungenderk1904kn` (used via the
  scratch copy `apaw1904.txt`). Müller died 1930. Pages: shabuhragan,
  turfan-gospel-epistles, turfan-cosmogony, turfan-hymnbook-m4,
  turfan-mani-legends, turfan-passion, turfan-hymns-prayers,
  turfan-fragments-2 to -4.
- **F. W. K. Müller, "Ein Doppelblatt aus einem manichäischen Hymnenbuch
  (Mahrnāmag)"**, Abh. Preuss. Akad. 1912, `abhandlungenderk1912akad`.
  Page: mahrnamag.
- **A. von Le Coq, "Türkische Manichaica aus Chotscho" I, II, III**, Abh.
  Preuss. Akad. 1911 (`abhandlungenderk1911akaduoft`), 1919
  (`abhandlungenderk1919akaduoft`), 1922 (`AbhandDerPreussAkadDerWissen1922`).
  Le Coq died 1930. Pages: turkish-manichaica-1, -2, -3a to -3e,
  turkish-sermon-light-nous, turkish-hymn-to-mani.
- **A. von Le Coq, SPAW 1908 and 1909** (`in.gov.ignca.26822`,
  `in.gov.ignca.26825`). Pages: zarathustra-in-babel, le-coq-1909.
- **Festschrift Vilhelm Thomsen** (Leipzig 1912), `festschriftvilhe00thom`:
  Le Coq's book fragment and Müller's "Hofstaat". Page: bogu-khan.
- **G. Flügel, *Mani, seine Lehre und seine Schriften*** (1862),
  `maniseinelehreu00manegoog`. Flügel died 1870. Pages: al-nadim-books,
  al-nadim-teaching.
- **J. Zycha, CSEL 25.2** (Vienna 1892), `CorpusScriptorumEcclesiasticorumLatin252`:
  Evodius, *De fide contra Manichaeos*. The Latin text is ancient; the
  edition is out of copyright. Page: evodius-quotations.
- **C. H. Beeson, *Hegemonius: Acta Archelai*** (GCS 16, 1906),
  `actaarchelaihrsg00hegeuoft`. Latin text ancient; Beeson died 1949, so
  the edition apparatus is EU public domain from 2020; the text translated
  is the ancient Latin. Page: acta-archelai.
- **M.-A. Kugener and F. Cumont, *Extrait de la CXXIIIe homélie de Sévère
  d'Antioche*** (Brussels 1912), `MN41725ucmf_0`. Cumont died 1947,
  Kugener 1941. Page: severus-quotations.
- **H. Pognon, *Inscriptions mandaïtes des coupes de Khouabir*** (Paris
  1898), `inscriptionsmand00pognuoft`, appendix on Theodore bar Konai.
  Pognon died 1921. Page: theodore-bar-konai.
- **Checked and not usable**: Allberry 1938 (`gtu_32400005908003_2`) and the
  Giversen facsimiles (`manichaeancoptic0001give`–`0004give`) are
  lending-only; Salemann 1904 (`einbruchstkmanic00sale`) gives no connected
  translation; Foy 1904 (SPAW, `in.gov.ignca.26815`) and Müller's *Uigurica*
  1908 and II (1910) are linguistic or Buddhist.
