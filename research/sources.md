# Sources

One entry per source: citation or link, date accessed, kind, what it
contributes, and how far to trust it.

- **NYPL, Catalog of Copyright Entries renewals** (transcription),
  github.com/NYPL/cce-renewals, files `data/1958-1.tsv` to `1961-1.tsv`.
  Accessed 2026-10-20. Primary record (a transcription of a government
  register). Contributes: Jackson, *Researches in Manichaeism* (1932) renewed
  R249768, 1960-01-04. Trust: high. A missing entry is weaker evidence than a
  found one.
- **Cambridge Core, JRAS 43.2 (April 1911)**, record for Le Coq's
  "Dr. Stein's Turkish Khuastuanift". Accessed 2026-10-20. Publisher
  catalogue. Contributes: the publication facts (pp. 277–314). Trust: high.
- **Radloff, review in *Mélanges asiatiques* 15 (1911–12)**,
  orientalstudies.ru PDF. Accessed 2026-10-20. Contemporary scholarly review.
  Contributes: confirms that Le Coq 1911 has an English translation and a
  supplement from Berlin fragments. Trust: high.
- **archive.org search**, accessed 2026-10-20. Scans found:
  `gtu_32400005908003_2` (Allberry 1938), `researchesinmani0000avwi` and
  `in.ernet.dli.2015.187154` (Jackson 1932), `maniseinelehreu00manegoog`
  (Flügel 1862). Trust: high for whether a scan exists, none for copyright.
- **Wikipedia, "Charles Allberry"**. Accessed 2026-10-20. Encyclopedia.
  Contributes: Allberry died in 1943. Trust: medium.
- **Library catalogues** (Columbia UP 1932 record for Jackson, with his dates
  1862–1937; Kohlhammer 1938 record for Allberry). Accessed 2026-10-20.
  Trust: high.
