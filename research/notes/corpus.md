# Corpus inventory

> Supporting note. The master list is `research/SCOPE.md`; this file
> has background on manuscripts and editions.

The surviving Manichaean scriptural, liturgical and doctrinal texts, grouped
by language and find. For each text the inventory gives the manuscripts, the
standard edition, the English translations, and how complete it is.

Citations come from the standard bibliography of the field. Entries marked
**[verify]** have a detail (a date, volume or page range) recalled from
memory that has not yet been checked against a catalogue. Copyright status is
covered in `copyright.md`.

## 1. Mani's own canon (lost as books, surviving in fragments)

The canon was seven works: the Living Gospel, the Treasure of Life, the
Pragmateia, the Book of Mysteries, the Book of Giants, the Epistles, and the
Psalms and Prayers. The Middle Persian Šābuhragān, written for Shapur I, and
the Ardahang (the picture book) sit alongside them. Kephalaia ch. 148 and the
Coptic Homilies list the canon.

| Work | What survives | Edition / translation |
|---|---|---|
| Living Gospel | Its opening, quoted in the Cologne Mani Codex (66–68); Middle Persian and Uyghur fragments; quotations in al-Bīrūnī | CMC (see §3); Sundermann, "Mani's 'Book of the Giants'…", and Turfan fragments **[verify]** |
| Treasure of Life | Quoted by Augustine (*De natura boni* 44) and Evodius; mentioned by al-Bīrūnī | Latin in CSEL 25; English in NPNF ser. 1 vol. 4 (1887) |
| Book of Giants | Middle Persian, Parthian, Sogdian and Uyghur fragments | Henning, "The Book of the Giants", BSOAS 11 (1943) 52–74; Reeves, *Jewish Lore in Manichaean Cosmogony* (1992); Morano (2016) **[verify]** |
| Epistles | The Epistula Fundamenti, quoted by Augustine; the Coptic Letters codex (Berlin, mostly lost after 1945); Kellis fragments | Augustine, *Contra epistulam fundamenti* (NPNF ser. 1 vol. 4); Gardner, Kellis Literary Texts 2 (2007) |
| Šābuhragān | Middle Persian Turfan fragments, mostly on eschatology; al-Bīrūnī quotes its opening | MacKenzie, "Mani's Šābuhragān", BSOAS 42 (1979) 500–534 and 43 (1980) 288–310 (text and English translation); Hutter (1992) |
| Pragmateia, Mysteries | Titles, chapter lists (al-Nadīm) and stray citations | al-Nadīm, *Fihrist* ch. 9 |

## 8. Latin and Arabic witnesses that preserve scripture

- **Augustine**: quotations from the Epistula Fundamenti and the Treasure of
  Life. NPNF ser. 1 vol. 4 (1887) is public domain.
- **Tebessa codex** (Latin, North Africa): fragments of a Manichaean treatise.
- **al-Bīrūnī**: quotes the Šābuhragān and the Living Gospel. Sachau, *The
  Chronology of Ancient Nations* (1879) and *Alberuni's India* (1888) are
  public domain.
- **Ibn al-Nadīm, *Fihrist***: Mani's doctrine, a list of his books, and
  quotations. Flügel, *Mani, seine Lehre und seine Schriften* (1862, German)
  is public domain. Dodge (1970, English) is under copyright.
- **Acta Archelai**: a hostile source, but it quotes Manichaean doctrine.
  Out of scope as scripture; it may go in an appendix.
