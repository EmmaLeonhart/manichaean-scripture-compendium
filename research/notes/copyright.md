# Copyright audit of older translations

Checked 2026-10-20. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (US) and read
worldwide, so a text goes in as-is only if it is public domain in **both the
US and the EU**.

The rules applied:

- **US:** everything published before 1931 is public domain (works from 1930
  entered on 2026-01-01). For 1931–1963 US publications, the work is public
  domain only if its copyright was not renewed in its 28th year. A foreign
  work from 1931 or later that was still protected in its home country on
  1996-01-01 had its US copyright restored by the URAA, for 95 years from
  publication.
- **EU and UK:** the author's life plus 70 years. A text is free from 1 January
  of the year after the 70th anniversary of the author's death.

Renewal records were searched in the NYPL transcription of the Catalog of
Copyright Entries (github.com/NYPL/cce-renewals), files for 1958–1961.

## What this means for the compendium

1. **Ready in English now:** the Xwāstwānīft (Le Coq 1911), the
   al-Bīrūnī quotations (Sachau 1879) and the Augustine quotations (NPNF
   1887). These can go online as-is, marked public domain.
2. **Fresh CC BY translations from public-domain French and German editions:**
   the Chinese Traité (Chavannes & Pelliot), the Fihrist account (Flügel),
   the early Turfan texts (Müller, Le Coq), and the Severus and Theodore bar
   Konai quotations (Cumont & Kugener). Each should be translated against the
   original-language text that these editions print, not only from their
   French or German.
3. **The big Coptic texts** (the Psalm-Book, Kephalaia and Homilies) have no
   free English translation. They need fresh translation from the Coptic. The
   Coptic text of Allberry 1938 is an edition of a 4th-century manuscript.
   Under US law the transcription of an ancient text is not copyrightable,
   though his restorations of lacunae arguably are. Translating directly from
   the Coptic, and marking where the translation follows an editor's
   restoration, is the safe route. The UNSAFE-TO-GUESS point is reproducing
   Allberry's English or his apparatus before 2034.
4. **The Cologne Mani Codex** has a Greek text published 1975–1988 and no free
   English translation. It needs a fresh translation from the Greek. The
   German scholarly-edition right (§70 UrhG, 25 years from publication) on the
   1988 edition expired in 2013.
